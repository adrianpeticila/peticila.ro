# Nemesis LI

A free LinkedIn profile grader. Paste your profile and the jobs you are actually targeting, and it returns a letter grade plus the **single biggest fix** between you and the shortlist. No account, no upload, no server. Everything runs in your browser and stays in your browser.

---

## The tool

**Inputs:**
- Full LinkedIn profile text (headline, about, experience, skills)
- 1-3 job descriptions you are targeting
- Optional: "Open to Work" toggle

**What it checks (heuristic, not scientific):**
- Headline length and keyword match with target JD
- Profile text length (sufficient for recruiter scanning)
- Keyword overlap between profile and target JDs
- Action verbs count (led, built, grew, etc.)
- Years of experience mentioned
- "Open to Work" presence on profile (if toggled)

**Output:**
- Letter grade A-F with estimated score out of 100
- Single biggest fix recommendation (free)
- Missing keywords from JDs worth adding
- Full fix list (5-7 items) behind a $19 unlock (placeholder - Stripe not yet wired)

**Data privacy:** All processing happens client-side. No data is sent to any server.

---

## How it works

1. **Paste your profile** - Copy your full LinkedIn profile text (headline, about, experience, skills)
2. **Add the jobs** - Paste 1-3 job descriptions you are targeting
3. **Get your grade + fix** - Receive an A-F grade and your single biggest improvement

---

## What this won't do

- This is a heuristic tool with simple keyword scoring. It does not understand nuance, context, or industry-specific terminology.
- It does not connect to the LinkedIn API. It only analyzes what you paste.
- The grading is a rough heuristic, not a scientific assessment.
- The full fix report behind the $19 paywall is not yet generated - the overlay is a placeholder.
