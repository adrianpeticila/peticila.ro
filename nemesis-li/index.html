    how it works

    three steps, no fluff.

    copy your full LinkedIn profile text. headline, about, experience, skills. no link needed, just the text.

    paste one to three job descriptions you are targeting. the grader compares your profile against the actual roles you want.

    you get an A-F letter grade based on keyword overlap, headline quality, summary completeness, and action verbs. the single biggest fix is free. the full list is unlocked at $19.

    honest limitations

    what this won't do.

    it is not a human recruiter reading your profile. it catches structural problems, not strategic ones.

    it does not simulate ATS parsing. it checks keyword overlap and profile structure.

    a high grade does not guarantee interviews. a low grade does not mean you are unqualified. it means your profile is not working hard enough.

    nothing is uploaded. nothing is stored. the grading runs entirely in your browser and dies when you close the tab.
