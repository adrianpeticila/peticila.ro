# peticila.ro

My name, my domain, my business card.

No framework, no build step, no cookie banner begging to follow you around. Hand-written HTML and CSS, plus just enough JavaScript to make the type fill the screen exactly.

This is the hub. Who I am, what I do, where to find me. Everything else I make links back here.

## Structure

- `/`: Bio, Uncliché Your Brand framework, "After working with me" outcomes, right now, press.
- `/manifesto/`: What I believe about marketing strategy, brand positioning, and why clarity beats volume.
- `/about/`: Full credentials, background, and 22-profile identity verification graph.
- `/work/`: 11 anonymized brand positioning engagements across fintech, SaaS, and healthtech.
- `/uncliche/`: Five-principle framework for brands absorbed by category-speak.
- `/press/`: Published interviews and media features.
- `/daemon/`: Machine-readable personal daemon, runtime directives, and JSON API for AI agents and digital assistants (JSON: `daemon-data.json`, plain text: `daemon.md`).
- `/brand-strategy/` & `/b2b-marketing-consultant/`: B2B strategy and positioning specialties.

**Stack:** HTML, CSS, vanilla JS. Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare DNS. A personal site doesn't need a bundler. It needs a point of view.

Built and maintained with an AI pair, supervised by [Gorgona](https://github.com/adrianpeticila/gorgona), the operating system that runs all my brands.

Live: [peticila.ro](https://peticila.ro)
